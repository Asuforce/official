# Surface brief: index.html

Scope: whole top page of asuforce.com. Visitor mode: Experience (portfolio; the work and the maker's craft lead).

Audience and job: engineers who judge craft; hiring managers who scan career and works fast. Content and facts are fixed (PRODUCT.md). English only. Dark only for now; theme toggle hidden, light side undesigned.

Pinned by the user: signature is numbered sections 01-05 with ruled stage-by-stage reveal; the one failure to avoid is hard to read. Revised after review: the giant name wordmark and the large ID heading were rejected; the first viewport is the user's ghost mascot (vectorized from public/img/obake.jpg, same art as their GitHub avatar) as a graphic.

Unresolved: light theme design; Positioning in PRODUCT.md.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is a numbered index of one engineer's work, opened by the maker's own ghost mascot drawn large as a graphic. Thin rules and numerals do the structuring that cards usually do. It refuses the dark-mesh hero with three equal feature cards.

OWN-WORLD (a quiet static starfield of 190 tiny white stars sits behind everything, behind the nav, text, ghost and moon): Black ground, white text, one acid accent #C0FE04 reserved for current location, the single primary action and focus. 1px white-at-20% rules, square corners, no shadows. Archivo variable (wide and thin for display, normal width for text), JetBrains Mono only for numerals and dates. Recognizable with the copy removed: a black page of ruled rows and one huge wide wordmark.

STORY: Visitor sees the name, understands the role from the bio line, scrolls a numbered index (Skills, Works, Career, Contact), and leaves knowing the work. Action: open GitHub or X.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Nav bar on top (small name left, links right, accent marks the current one). The ghost mascot is a fixed element: here a very large presence peeking in from the right edge of the first viewport, cropped by the edge and leaning in (opacity 0.2, behind the text), original colors. The space above the 01 row holds the first bio sentence as a large display lead (10+ years in DevOps and SRE., up to 88px, uppercase, thin). One ruled row at the bottom: 01 marker, the h1 name line (Sun (@asuforce)) at 24px max, and the rest of the bio at 17px. No large ID heading. In later sections the same ghost follows the reader, moving to the right edge with a different tilt and pupil direction per section. In Contact it returns to a large faint watermark like the top. At the very end of the page a geometric lunar surface (arc of ground, flat ellipse craters) fills the footer, and the ghost lands firmly on it: opaque white in front of the ground, feet planted below the horizon, a black contact shadow, a squash on impact and a ring spreading across the ground.

FORM: User-pinned direction from DESIGN.md (Marathon-derived black plus neon, Figure-style thin caps); no roll run, seed key: none (pinned).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
